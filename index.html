Retail Weekly Sales Analysis: Exploratory Data Analysis and Statistical Insights

This project analyzes weekly retail sales data to identify the key factors that influence sales performance and uncover actionable business insights. The analysis combines exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature engineering, statistical testing, and machine learning techniques to evaluate the impact of seasonality, promotions, holidays, and store characteristics on weekly sales. Using Linear Regression and Random Forest models, the project explores sales forecasting capabilities while providing data-driven recommendations to support retail planning, inventory management, and promotional strategies.

Python | Data cleaning | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) | Feature Engineering | Statistical Hypothesis Testing | Predictive Modeling | Data Visualization | Business Insight Generation

Project Objective

To identify the key factors influencing weekly retail sales performance, evaluate the impact of holidays, promotions, and regional differences, and generate actionable insights that support forecasting, inventory planning, and data-driven retail decision-making.

Business Questions Addressed

  • What factors most strongly influence weekly retail sales?
  • Do holidays and promotional markdowns significantly increase sales performance?
  • Are there meaningful differences in sales behavior across regions?
  • How predictable are weekly sales using historical operational data?
  • Which variables should decision-makers prioritize when planning inventory, promotions, and staffing?
  • How can predictive models support retail forecasting and strategic planning?

Methodology

  • Data Loading and Preparation (Python): cleaning, validation, and structuring of datasets (features, sales, and store).
  • Feature Engineering: to improve analytical depth and forecasting accuracy, new variables were created from existing data (Time Features, Business Features, Growth Metrics, Lag Features and Rolling Features).
  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): The EDA phase focused on uncovering sales trends, seasonal behaviors, promotional impacts, and regional differences to generate initial business insights and guide subsequent statistical and predictive analyses.
  • Statistical Analysis: Statistical analysis was conducted to validate business assumptions, quantify relationships between variables, and identify significant factors influencing weekly retail sales.
  • Predictive Modeling: Predictive modeling was used to estimate future sales performance, compare model effectiveness, and uncover the variables with the greatest impact on weekly retail sales.

Business Insights

1. Seasonal Sales Patterns

Weekly sales exhibit strong seasonality, with demand fluctuating significantly throughout the year and peaking during specific periods.

2. Holiday Impact on Sales

Holiday weeks consistently outperform non-holiday periods, confirming the significant influence of seasonal events on retail performance.

3. Regional Performance Differences

Statistical analysis revealed meaningful differences in sales performance across regions, highlighting the need for localized business strategies.

4. Promotions Drive Sales Growth

Promotional markdowns showed a positive relationship with sales performance, demonstrating their effectiveness in stimulating customer demand.

5. Historical Sales are Strong Predictors

Recent sales behavior, particularly rolling averages and lagged sales, emerged as the most influential factors for forecasting future sales.

6. Reliable Forecasting Capability

Predictive models were able to explain approximately 63% of sales variability, demonstrating the potential of data-driven forecasting for retail planning and decision-making.

Conclusion

This analysis revealed that weekly retail sales are primarily influenced by historical sales behavior, promotional strategies, seasonality, and regional factors. Statistical testing confirmed the significant impact of holiday periods and regional differences on sales performance, while predictive modeling demonstrated the potential to forecast future sales using operational and promotional variables. Overall, the results highlight the importance of leveraging historical trends and business intelligence to support retail planning and decision-making.

Business Recommendations

  • Implement region-specific sales and marketing strategies to address differences in customer behavior and sales performance.
  • Strengthen inventory planning and resource allocation ahead of holiday periods to capitalize on increased demand.
  • Use promotional markdowns strategically during low-demand periods to drive sales and improve revenue performance.
  • Incorporate historical sales indicators into forecasting processes to enhance prediction accuracy.
  • Prioritize investment and operational support for high-performing stores and departments.
  • Closely monitor regions with higher sales volatility to reduce operational risk and improve planning efficiency.

1. Weekly Sales Over Time

Sales exhibit marked seasonality and significant fluctuations over time.

2. Holidays vs Non-Holiday Weekly Sales

Weeks with holidays generate significantly higher sales than regular weeks.

3. Weekly Sales by Region

Significant differences in sales performance across regions suggest opportunities for region-specific business strategies.

It allows for the visual and intuitive identification of seasonal patterns and months of peak demand.

5. Markdown Impact on Weekly Sales

Showed how promotional activities influence customer demand and revenue generation.

6. Feature Importance Analysis

Identified rolling sales averages, lagged sales, and markdown activity as the strongest predictors of future sales performance.

RappiPlus Subscription Analytics: Revenue, Retention and Funnel Optimization

This project analyzes the performance of the RappiPlus subscription service to evaluate user behavior, profitability, customer retention, and conversion efficiency. The analysis combines Python, SQL, cohort analysis, funnel analytics, and A/B testing techniques to identify growth opportunities and optimize the customer journey. Through a business-oriented analytical approach, the project transforms operational and behavioral data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making and sustainable business growth.

Python | SQL | Data Cleaning | Funnel Analysis | Cohort Analysis | A/B Testing | Business Analytics | Dashboarding

Project Objective

To evaluate the performance of the RappiPlus subscription service by analyzing profitability, customer behavior, retention patterns, and conversion efficiency in order to identify growth opportunities and support data-driven business decisions.

Business Questions Addressed

  • Is the subscription model financially sustainable and profitable?
  • At which stages of the customer journey are users abandoning the purchase process?
  • Do subscribers return and generate long-term business value?
  • How effectively does the conversion funnel transform visitors into paying customers?
  • Do product changes have a measurable impact on user behavior and business performance?
  • Which actions could improve conversion, retention, and profitability?

Methodology

  • Data Preparation (Python): data cleaning, validation, and structuring of datasets including orders, catalog information, and marketing data.
  • Profitability Analysis: calculation of key business KPIs such as Revenue, Cost, and Profit to evaluate financial performance and identify high-value customer segments.
  • Conversion Funnel Analysis (SQL): evaluation of the customer journey to identify drop-off points and conversion bottlenecks.
  • Cohort Analysis (SQL): examination of customer retention and behavioral patterns over time to assess subscription loyalty and engagement.
  • A/B Testing (Python): statistical evaluation of product changes to measure their impact on user behavior and business outcomes.
  • Dashboarding: communication of findings through visual analytics and business-focused reporting.

Business Insights

1. Data Quality Supports Reliable Decision-Making

Data preparation and validation ensured a consistent analytical foundation, enabling trustworthy business insights and more accurate performance evaluation.

2. Profitability Varies Across Customer Segments

Profitability analysis revealed differences in value generation among customer groups, highlighting opportunities to prioritize high-performing segments.

3. Critical Funnel Drop-Off Points Were Identified

Funnel analysis exposed stages of the subscription process with significant customer abandonment, providing clear opportunities to improve conversion performance.

4. Retention Patterns Influence Long-Term Value

Cohort analysis uncovered behavioral trends that help explain how customer engagement evolves over time and contributes to recurring revenue.

5. Product Changes Can Be Quantifiably Measured

A/B testing enabled objective evaluation of product modifications, supporting evidence-based decisions rather than assumptions.

6. Data-Driven Opportunities for Growth

The analysis identified actionable opportunities to strengthen conversion rates, improve retention, and maximize profitability across the subscription business model.

Conclusion

This analysis provided a comprehensive understanding of the RappiPlus subscription service, from customer acquisition through retention and profitability. The results demonstrated the value of combining behavioral analytics, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, and experimentation to support business growth initiatives and optimize customer experience.

Business Recommendations

  • Optimize funnel stages with the highest customer abandonment rates.
  • Implement retention initiatives targeted at cohorts with declining engagement.
  • Prioritize customer segments that generate the highest profitability.
  • Expand the use of A/B testing to validate future product and marketing decisions.
  • Monitor funnel performance and customer lifetime value as core business metrics.
  • Use behavioral insights to personalize customer experiences and subscription offerings.

2. Cohort Retention Analysis

Showed how customer retention evolves over time and highlighted recurring engagement patterns.

3. Business KPI Dashboard

Presented key performance indicators related to revenue, costs, profitability, and subscription performance.

Análisis de datos operativos para optimizar recursos

En este proyecto analicé datos de calidad de soluble, mantenimiento y disposición para identificar desperdicio y fugas en procesos de maquinado. A través de técnicas de limpieza, análisis exploratorio y modelado, se detectaron oportunidades de recuperación del recurso, logrando reducir costos operativos y mejorar la sostenibilidad del proceso.

EXCEL | POWER BI | RECOLECCIÓN DE DATOS | LIMPIEZA DE DATOS | TRANSFORMACIÓN DE DATOS | ANÁLISIS EXPLORATORIO | VISUALIZACIÓN

Problemas de negocio a resolver

  • Determinar qué parámetros de calidad del soluble permiten su reciclaje.
  • Identificar la proporción de soluble que se dispone sin tratamiento previo.
  • Detectar prácticas operativas que generan desperdicio innecesario.
  • Estimar el ahorro potencial al recuperar soluble mediante filtración o reciclaje.

Metodología

  • Recolección de datos:
    • Datos obtenidos de registros físicos en planta:
    - Calidad del soluble
    - Mantenimientos predictivos y correctivos
    - Manifiestos de disposición
  • Preprocesamiento:
    • - Limpieza y estandarización de datos
    - Eliminación de inconsistencias
    - Verificación de duplicados
  • Análisis y modelado:
    • - Análisis esploratorio (EDA):evaluación de patrones de calidad y consumo
    - Modelado predictivo: estimación de ahorro potencial mediante simulación y regresión
    - Clustering: clasificación del soluble según su nivel de contaminación

Principales resultados

1. Factores críticos de contaminación

Se identificó que la contaminación del soluble está relacionada principalmente con prácticas operativas, como:
- Presencia de basura
- Residuos de desengrasante
- Materiales ajenos al proceso
👉 Esto acelera la degradación del soluble y aumenta su disposición.

2. Impacto de fugas en el consumo

- Máquinas con historial de fugas presentan mayor consumo de soluble
- Existe mayor variabilidad en la concentración del fluido
👉 Las fugas son un factor clave en el desperdicio del recurso.

3. Oportunidad de recuperación

- Parte significativa del soluble puede recuperarse mediante filtración o separación
- Existe potencial de reducción en costos de compra y disposición

4. Relación entre mantenimiento y eficiencia

Máquinas con mantenimiento predictivo constante tienen:
- Menor variabilidad en calidad
- Menor volumen de disposición
👉 El mantenimiento impacta directamente la eficiencia operativa.

Conclusión (impacto del proyecto)

El análisis permitió identificar oportunidades claras para reducir desperdicios y costos asociados al uso del soluble. La implementación de estrategias de reciclaje y mejora operativa puede generar ahorros significativos y contribuir a procesos más sostenibles.

Recomendaciones

  • Estandarizar prácticas de limpieza para reducir contaminación
  • Implementar sistemas de reciclaje (filtración, centrífugas, separación de aceites)
  • Priorizar mantenimiento predictivo en máquinas críticas
  • Monitorear indicadores clave:
    • - pH
    - concentración
    - conductividad
    - % de contaminación
    - volumen dispuesto por máquina

    Visualizaciones clave

    1. Parámetros de calidad por sección

    Análisis de concentración y pH que permitió identificar desviaciones y deterioro del soluble.

    2. Volumen de disposición por máquina

    Identificación de equipos con mayor desperdicio y posibles causas.

    3. Tipos de contaminación

    Clasificación de contaminantes para definir estrategias específicas de recuperación.

    4. Distribución de mantenimiento
