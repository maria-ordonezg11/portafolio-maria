Retail Weekly Sales Analysis: Exploratory Data Analysis and Statistical Insights

This project analyzes weekly retail sales data to identify the key factors that influence sales performance and uncover actionable business insights. The analysis combines exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature engineering, statistical testing, and machine learning techniques to evaluate the impact of seasonality, promotions, holidays, and store characteristics on weekly sales. Using Linear Regression and Random Forest models, the project explores sales forecasting capabilities while providing data-driven recommendations to support retail planning, inventory management, and promotional strategies.

Python | Data cleaning | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) | Feature Engineering | Statistical Hypothesis Testing | Predictive Modeling | Data Visualization | Business Insight Generation

Project Objective

To identify the key factors influencing weekly retail sales performance, evaluate the impact of holidays, promotions, and regional differences, and generate actionable insights that support forecasting, inventory planning, and data-driven retail decision-making.

Business Questions Addressed

  • What factors most strongly influence weekly retail sales?
  • Do holidays and promotional markdowns significantly increase sales performance?
  • Are there meaningful differences in sales behavior across regions?
  • How predictable are weekly sales using historical operational data?
  • Which variables should decision-makers prioritize when planning inventory, promotions, and staffing?
  • How can predictive models support retail forecasting and strategic planning?

Methodology

  • Data Loading and Preparation (Python): cleaning, validation, and structuring of datasets (features, sales, and store).
  • Feature Engineering: to improve analytical depth and forecasting accuracy, new variables were created from existing data (Time Features, Business Features, Growth Metrics, Lag Features and Rolling Features).
  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): The EDA phase focused on uncovering sales trends, seasonal behaviors, promotional impacts, and regional differences to generate initial business insights and guide subsequent statistical and predictive analyses.
  • Statistical Analysis: Statistical analysis was conducted to validate business assumptions, quantify relationships between variables, and identify significant factors influencing weekly retail sales.
  • Predictive Modeling: Predictive modeling was used to estimate future sales performance, compare model effectiveness, and uncover the variables with the greatest impact on weekly retail sales.

Business Insights

1. Seasonal Sales Patterns

Weekly sales exhibit strong seasonality, with demand fluctuating significantly throughout the year and peaking during specific periods.

2. Holiday Impact on Sales

Holiday weeks consistently outperform non-holiday periods, confirming the significant influence of seasonal events on retail performance.

3. Regional Performance Differences

Statistical analysis revealed meaningful differences in sales performance across regions, highlighting the need for localized business strategies.

4. Promotions Drive Sales Growth

Promotional markdowns showed a positive relationship with sales performance, demonstrating their effectiveness in stimulating customer demand.

5. Historical Sales are Strong Predictors

Recent sales behavior, particularly rolling averages and lagged sales, emerged as the most influential factors for forecasting future sales.

6. Reliable Forecasting Capability

Predictive models were able to explain approximately 63% of sales variability, demonstrating the potential of data-driven forecasting for retail planning and decision-making.

Conclusion

This analysis revealed that weekly retail sales are primarily influenced by historical sales behavior, promotional strategies, seasonality, and regional factors. Statistical testing confirmed the significant impact of holiday periods and regional differences on sales performance, while predictive modeling demonstrated the potential to forecast future sales using operational and promotional variables. Overall, the results highlight the importance of leveraging historical trends and business intelligence to support retail planning and decision-making.

Business Recommendations

  • Implement region-specific sales and marketing strategies to address differences in customer behavior and sales performance.
  • Strengthen inventory planning and resource allocation ahead of holiday periods to capitalize on increased demand.
  • Use promotional markdowns strategically during low-demand periods to drive sales and improve revenue performance.
  • Incorporate historical sales indicators into forecasting processes to enhance prediction accuracy.
  • Prioritize investment and operational support for high-performing stores and departments.
  • Closely monitor regions with higher sales volatility to reduce operational risk and improve planning efficiency.

Sales exhibit marked seasonality and significant fluctuations over time.

2. Holidays vs Non-Holiday Weekly Sales

Weeks with holidays generate significantly higher sales than regular weeks.

3. Weekly Sales by Region

Significant differences in sales performance across regions suggest opportunities for region-specific business strategies.

It allows for the visual and intuitive identification of seasonal patterns and months of peak demand.

Showed how promotional activities influence customer demand and revenue generation.

Operational Resource Optimization: Manufacturing Efficiency and Cost Reduction Analysis

This project focuses on the analysis of coolant quality, maintenance records, and disposal data within machining operations to identify waste sources, leakage-related losses, and opportunities for resource recovery. Through data collection, exploratory analysis, predictive modeling, and operational performance evaluation, the project sought to reduce operational costs, improve resource utilization, and support sustainability initiatives. By combining technical manufacturing knowledge with data analytics, the analysis translated operational data into actionable recommendations that support efficiency improvements and long-term cost savings.

Excel | Power BI | Data Collection | Data Cleaning | Data Transformation | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) | Predictive Modeling | Data Visualization

Project Objective

To identify opportunities for resource recovery and operational efficiency by analyzing coolant quality, maintenance activities, and disposal practices, ultimately reducing waste, lowering operating costs, and supporting more sustainable manufacturing processes.

Business Questions Addressed

  • Which coolant quality parameters determine whether the resource can be recycled or reused?
  • How much coolant is disposed of without prior treatment or recovery?
  • Which operational practices contribute most significantly to resource waste?
  • How do equipment leaks influence coolant consumption and process efficiency?
  • What cost savings could be achieved through filtration, recycling, or process improvements?
  • How does maintenance performance affect coolant quality and disposal volumes?

Methodology

  • Data Collection: gathered operational information from manufacturing records, including coolant quality measurements, predictive and corrective maintenance activities, and disposal documentation.
  • Data Preparation: data cleaning, standardization, duplicate verification, and correction of inconsistencies to ensure analytical reliability.
  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): evaluation of coolant quality trends, consumption patterns, contamination sources, and disposal behavior.
  • Predictive Modeling: estimation of potential savings through filtration, recycling, and process optimization scenarios.
  • Clustering Analysis: classification of coolant conditions based on contamination levels to support recovery and recycling strategies.
  • Business Intelligence & Visualization: development of dashboards and visual analyses to communicate operational findings and support decision-making.

Business Insights

1. Contamination is Primarily Driven by Operational Practices

Analysis revealed that coolant contamination is largely associated with process-related factors such as debris, degreaser residues, and foreign materials entering the system, accelerating coolant degradation and increasing disposal requirements.

2. Equipment Leaks Significantly Increase Resource Consumption

Machines with a history of leaks consistently showed higher coolant consumption and greater variability in fluid concentration, demonstrating the operational impact of equipment integrity issues.

3. Resource Recovery Opportunities Exist

A significant portion of disposed coolant showed potential for recovery through filtration, separation, or recycling processes, creating opportunities for substantial cost reduction.

4. Maintenance Directly Influences Operational Efficiency

Equipment receiving consistent predictive maintenance exhibited more stable coolant quality and lower disposal volumes, highlighting the importance of preventive operational strategies.

5. Sustainability and Cost Reduction Objectives Align

Reducing coolant waste not only lowers procurement and disposal costs but also supports environmental sustainability initiatives within manufacturing operations.

6. Data Analytics Can Support Resource Optimization

The analysis demonstrated how operational data can be leveraged to identify inefficiencies, prioritize improvement initiatives, and support continuous process optimization.

Conclusion

This project identified clear opportunities to improve manufacturing efficiency by reducing coolant waste, preventing losses related to equipment leaks, and increasing resource recovery. The results demonstrate how data-driven operational analysis can generate measurable business value through cost savings, process optimization, and sustainability improvements.

Business Recommendations

  • Standardize cleaning procedures to minimize coolant contamination.
  • Implement filtration, centrifugation, and oil-separation systems to maximize coolant recovery.
  • Prioritize predictive maintenance programs for critical equipment.
  • Monitor coolant quality indicators such as pH, concentration, conductivity, and contamination levels.
  • Track coolant disposal volumes at the machine level to identify inefficiencies.
  • Incorporate sustainability metrics into operational performance reviews.

Key Visualizations

1. Coolant Quality Parameters by Production Area

Identified variations in concentration and pH levels that indicate quality deterioration and opportunities for process improvements.

2. Coolant Disposal Volume by Machine

Highlighted equipment generating the largest amounts of waste and potential root causes of resource loss.

3. Contamination Classification Analysis

Categorized contamination sources to support targeted recovery and waste-reduction strategies.

4. Maintenance Performance Distribution

Compared predictive and corrective maintenance activities to evaluate their relationship with operational efficiency.
